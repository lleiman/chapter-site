02 / RANKING MODEL

Relevance is not probability.

The proposed 100-point score combines skill match (25), eligibility (20), economics (15), source reliability (15), deadline (10), career preference (10) and available competitive evidence (5). Hard eligibility failures override the score.

SKILLS 25
ELIGIBILITY 20
ECONOMICS 15
SOURCE 15
TIMING 10
GOALS 10
DATA 5

The weights are a product hypothesis. No empirical success probability is calculated.

Submit your CV for the pilot.

Early-access intake for future profile matching. A human-readable CV is stored privately for up to 30 days. The live site does not yet parse your CV with AI, send applications or match you to a live feed.
